01 — Design

A fold that reads as one object.

Two rigid halves share a single vertical axis. The display crosses it without a break, so the silhouette stays symmetrical and the device never reads as two panels bolted together.

  1. 001

    Hinge

    A full-height two-barrel spine carries both halves from flat to closed, with detents at 30, 60, 90, 120 and 150 degrees. The presentation angle is 158 degrees.

  2. 002

    Display

    A 7.78-inch active area crosses the fold over a 10 mm flexible bridge. The crease is a soft optical seam, never a hard line.

  3. 003

    Finish

    Brushed titanium, matte rear glass and a polished spine. Four colourways shift the rail, rear glass, plateau and interface accent together.
